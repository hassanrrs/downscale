Privacy Policy

Application: DownScale: Video Resizer

Developer: Splint Media

Last Updated: November 5, 2026

Splint Media ("we", "our", or "us") operates the DownScale: Video Resizer mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our App.

Summary: DownScale: Video Resizer processes videos locally on your device hardware. Your video and media files are never uploaded to any remote server or third party.

1. Information Collection & Storage

Local Media Processing (100% On-Device)

All video resizing, compression, and transcoding tasks are handled locally on your device hardware (utilizing hardware-accelerated Media3 Transformer APIs).

  • Your videos, audio files, and exported media never leave your device.
  • We do not upload, transmit, store, or monitor your personal media files on any remote servers or cloud systems.

Personal Information

The App does not require registration, accounts, or logins. We do not collect personally identifiable information such as names, phone numbers, or physical addresses.

2. Device Permissions

To deliver its functionality, the App requests access to specific device features:

  • Media & Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to allow you to select video files from your device library for downscaling and compression.
  • Storage Save Access (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): Used solely to write and export compressed video files back into your device storage.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET): Used strictly to retrieve advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The underlying video compression tool operates completely offline without needing network access.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App does not incorporate analytics suites or crash-reporting telemetry SDKs (such as Google Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics). The only integrated third-party network is:

  • Google AdMob: Facilitates ad delivery within the App. Google AdMob may process device identifiers, advertising IDs, IP addresses, and diagnostic info in accordance with Google's Privacy Policies:
    Google Privacy Policy • AdMob Privacy & Data Practices

4. Data Retention & User Rights

Because we do not collect or store user data on external servers, there are no remote databases or profiles associated with you.

You retain full control over locally stored data. You can erase all application preferences and local temporary cache at any point through your system settings (Settings > Apps > DownScale: Video Resizer > Storage > Clear Data/Cache) or simply by uninstalling the application.

5. Children's Privacy

Our service is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this URL with a revised "Last Updated" timestamp.
